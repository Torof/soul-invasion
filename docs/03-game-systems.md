# 03 — Game systems

*Validated principles. Numbers and detailed rules will come with the versions. Updated on 13 November 2026 with the project lead's design decisions of 12 and 13 November; what was proposed but not decided is in `06`.*

## 1. The soul

- **The player is a soul**, not a character.
- The soul has: soul points, karma, powers, titles, a history of all its lives.
- It **lives above the worlds**: it can migrate from one world to another.
- Any inhabitant can become a soul's body. Players and NPCs are the same kind of inhabitant; only the pilot changes.
- **Players cannot be told apart from NPCs.** Finding one another out, to kill or to cooperate, is one of the great engines of the game (12 November 2026).

## 1b. Soul and body: who decides

*Decided on 12 November 2026; the architecture is D13 and D12.*

- **Three voices decide for the body, in order**: the **soul**, when the player gives orders; the **plan**, what the soul has arranged ("tomorrow, fish, then smoke the fish"), which **fades with time**, and faster in a body of strong Willpower; the **body's own mind**: its habits, the character the soul has forged, its Willpower.
- **Habit**: what the soul did often becomes the body's routine (hunting with Aka every morning).
- **Forged character**: the soul's acts change the body's traits. A peaceful baker led to war becomes violent and conquering, and carries on that way when the soul is away.
- **Willpower gives scope, not randomness**: the body always chooses what resembles it. Little Willpower, small decisions and routine; much Willpower, large ones: it attacked the next town, married the king's daughter, overthrew the chief. One is surprised by the scale, never by incoherence.
- **The great lure and the great risk**: a body of strong Willpower often already has an impressive life (warrior, king, scholar). One wants it badly, and holds it less well.
- **Every situation, by the same rule**: idle for about a minute, the body carries on what it was doing, or chooses for itself if it has nothing to do (back to camp, exploring, setting a snare); reflexes always act (fleeing a wolf). A short disconnection: the plan holds. A long absence: the plan has faded and the body lives its life; the apprentice smith becomes a smith; if the soul never returns, the body lives on, then dies. On return, a **journal** tells the marking moments: it is the *body's* journal, made of its memories, and it can be wrong (if it believed a false rumour).
- **Plans**: a short list of intentions, with possible prohibitions ("do not leave the camp"); the thought bubble says "I am going fishing, as planned". A plan fades gently, never with a cut-off, and a long absence always leaves the body to live. Lengths and the screen form are open (`06`).
- **The soul's mastery**: no levels, but a true progression, **common to all souls** and not a power. A growing soul can take ever stronger bodies (more Willpower, so more destiny) and hold them better: longer, richer, firmer plans; less desynchronisation. The heart of the system: a body's Willpower is both **the treasure and the risk**. One may leave a body of great Willpower three days and come back to an extraordinary decision. It balances itself; newcomers need no shield.

## 1c. Sleep, and what the soul does while the body sleeps

*Decided on 12 November 2026.*

- **The player chooses when the body sleeps.** Putting it off raises fatigue: less strength, irritation, slower reactions; at the end, the body falls asleep where it stands.
- **Desynchronisation**: a very tired body obeys the soul badly: a gesture comes late, a blow misses, it answers curtly, it refuses an exhausting order. A body of strong Willpower resists more. Never a screen effect (no blur, no inverted controls).
- **While the body sleeps**, the player may disconnect (the body sleeps as without them); **go out as an invisible ghost**, only within the area already revealed around the body (the fog does not recede): spy, watch, see who steals from the store at night. On waking, the body keeps what the ghost saw **as a dream**: a belief the player holds, but "I saw it in a dream" is no witness. The player may also read **the book of the soul** (its past lives, its deeds, its encounters, what the world still tells of them; each life a summary sized by its deeds, D17), or **plan** the next day.
- A night of six game hours lasts about thirty real minutes: read, spy, plan, then a real pause.

## 2. Reincarnation

- **Soul points** are gained or lost according to how one lives and dies: a fulfilled life, martyrdom, influence, wealth, foundations = bonus; abandonment, hasty reincarnation = penalty. A victim must not be punished for their death (to be refined).
- **Incarnation cost** = Willpower of the targeted body. A beginner is reborn as a slave, a fisher or a baker; a powerful soul can aim for a noble child, or even higher.
- **Heirs**: you can be reborn into your lineage if you have prepared an heir; their value depends on what you invested (education, businesses, contacts). Educating an heir raises their Willpower, which protects them from other souls.
- **Lineage theft** is possible by other souls. Counters: a hidden heir, hidden treasures known only to you, a will in favor of an organization, powers.
- **The choice of rebirth is the choice of difficulty**: an isolated village, a large protected but rigid city, a wild frontier.
- **Information**: the more powerful the soul, the better it perceives the risks of a place of rebirth.
- **The dynasty**: a soul is reborn in the same world, at the time it died (dead in year 50, reborn in year 50 or 51), with everything already built. It can prepare an heir and be reborn into the line.
- **Past lives are secret**, discoverable through investigation (in-game and out-of-game). **Reputation stays with the body**; the soul leaves clean. But **the raw acts change karma**, which follows the soul from life to life (confirmed 12 November 2026).
- **Reincarnating incognito** is possible; the village forgets, karma does not.

## 3. Soul powers and titles

- **Powers**: rare, slow to develop, each one closes other doors. A power belongs to one soul, which develops it itself. ~~They only act at death and at rebirth.~~ **Each power has its own moment** (12 November 2026): some act at birth and death, others during life, others on particular things. Mastery (section 1b) is not a power.
  - *Bloodline Bond*: nobody else can incarnate into your lineage; trade-offs to be defined.
  - *Dethroner* (provisional name; *Vampire* until 12 November 2026): can seize the heirs of others. The name *Vampire* now belongs to a proposed power of a sleeping body (`06`).
  - *Lucky*: random rebirth, from the worst to the best.
- **Passive titles** earned through one's deeds, not chosen. A dark example: *Chain of Hell*, for gratuitous relentlessness (harming without purpose, endlessly preying on the weak): forced rebirth into hard lives. The villain who plays their role is not punished; excess is. The exact rules deliberately remain mysterious.
- **Paths of elevation** (inner alchemy): the soul grows stronger through the way one lives (builder, sovereign, sage, conqueror, merchant…), including a dark path. Soul strength, knowledge, skills and powers form an alchemy unique to each player.

## 4. The summits

- **Demon Lords (8 seats)**: tied to mortal life. Longer life, permanent bonus to the soul. Dark rituals possible to prolong life, leaving traces. A race against rivals and against time; preparing one's succession and one's return is the strategy.
- **World Emperor (1 seat)**: mortal, bearer of the Aura of Destiny. Major advantages to the soul and to reincarnation, according to a **hidden tree of possibilities**.
- The more powerful a soul, the more **exposed** it is.

## 5. NPCs

- Every NPC has: **needs** (hunger, cold, fatigue, safety), **character**, **Willpower**, **opinions** about the people they know, **memories**.
- **Decisions**: every NPC weighs their options and chooses what makes sense to them. Extreme acts (assassinating a tyrant) emerge from character, memories and opportunity, without a script.
- **Reactions to power**: a bad leader provokes flight, conspiracy, exile, coups, assassination. NPCs vote with their feet.
- **Groups**: families, tribes, villages have a collective mood and make collective decisions (submit, go to war, flee, ally).
- **Progress** (amended 12 November 2026): NPCs progress by the **transmission of knowledge**, among themselves and with players, more than by imitating players. Players are more productive: a region with players moves faster and further than a region of NPCs alone, so the world evolves differently according to where the players are, on purpose. NPCs alone still move forward, more slowly (how far is open, `06`).
- **Refusing an order** (12 November 2026): an NPC can refuse the order of a chief, player or not. It is the base of the game. The chief may then kill, banish or imprison, or accept; a rebellion may be born; the others' trust in the chief, or in the one who refused, moves.
- **Strong personalities** (12 November 2026): a thin share of the people, under one in fifty, in craft and in power. They change the conditions of a society rather than replace them: the land, the stored surplus and the cost of leaving say whether a kingdom is possible; strong personalities say when, who and in what form. Civil ones make the surplus and the knowledge; political ones turn it into structure, and that is where societies collapse. Only what was passed on remains: a process learnt, a custom, an institution, a work (a granary, a road), never a coded bonus.
- **Awakenings** (12 November 2026): Willpower also seeds at birth, at a random rate that strong events raise, but most strong personalities **awaken in an existing life**: an ordeal forges a few and breaks many, a project grows. Only those directly touched (actors, witnesses, close family) can awaken; no oracle picks them. A child's Willpower returns towards the average: weak kings are born on their own.
- **Big beasts are individuals** (12 November 2026): deer, wolves, bears, boars are born when someone sees them, live their natural life, then disappear; the wounded wolf stays *that* wolf ("it's him again, he limps"). Small game (hares, fish, birds) stays a count by region.
- NPCs can **found their own villages** and hold any position, including positions of power.
- **Romantic relationships**, couples, births, aging, death.
- **Attachment**: you grow attached to NPCs through shared history.

## 5b. Combat

- Slow real time by intentions: an engaged body commits one gesture per tempo (about a second); between tempos the player reads and chooses. No reflexes, no hit points, no battle mode.
- Gestures (strike, feint, parry, dodge, shove, break off) with directions and target limbs, three guards; simultaneous resolution, moves hidden until they land, wind-ups readable according to skill.
- Everything computed from content: weapon parts, protection by limb, skill, condition, facing, measure, numbers.
- Wounds by limb that bleed, hamper, heal and scar; death from a mortal wound or exhausted vitality; a corpse is an item.
- Assassination is combat against someone unaware. War is the same system at a hundred bodies, with orders, morale and fatigue.
- On the client, an engagement window that is a view of the same world; commands composable by the player, never conditional. Architecture decision D33.

## 6. Interacting with NPCs

- Every interaction goes through an **intention**: offer, help, ask, threaten, court, convince, lie…
- **Evolution**:
  1. Stone Age: actions and gestures (consistent with the lore).
  2. Later: NPCs who express themselves in their own words (generative AI).
  3. Eventually: free conversation, translated into intentions.
- **The AI never decides** on the NPC's behalf; it only helps them express themselves.
- **Speech in the Stone Age** (confirmed 12 November 2026): gestures and intentions (offer, help, ask, threaten, lie), composed with a **sentence builder** that also says what one asserts. NPCs answer with varied prepared sentences. No language model. Later, a free model specialised on the game's vocabulary, ideally on the player's machine (the server always checks again): no subscription, no price per use.
- **Unmasking** (12 November 2026): one is unmasked by what one says and does, not by how one types: not recognising one's own mother, asking one's brother's name, suddenly knowing how to smoke meat without having learnt it. **Others see the sentence the character said**, never the player's raw text, which would unmask every player who speaks and punish spelling or a foreign mother tongue. A skilful player can hide; a clumsy one gives themselves away.

## 7. Prestige, witnesses, suspicion

- **Prestige** = the sum of NPC opinions. Feeding the tribe, hunting well, inventing raise it; killing a member destroys it. Banishment is possible, with hostility upon return.
- **Witnesses**: every action has witnesses, every witness keeps a memory of it. An act without a witness does not exist for the world. Witnesses choose: stay silent, profit, denounce.
- **Suspicion**: personal, born from strange behavior. Its consequences depend on the local culture.

## 7b. Who knows what

*The invisible heart of the game (made precise on 12 November 2026).* Prestige, witnesses, banishment, the Emperor's Aura, investigations into past lives, secrets, lies and even the transmission of techniques rest on one thing: everyone knows only what they saw or were told.

- **NPCs act on what they believe, never on what is true.** A belief is a short sentence ("Brann killed the miller's son, at the ford, yesterday") with its **source** ("I saw", "Aka says", "people say"), a confidence and a date.
- **Information travels on foot**: a cry, a messenger, a merchant, a refugee. The next valley learns of the king's death days later.
- **An NPC judges before believing**: who tells me? Does it contradict what I saw? Is it plausible? Do I want to believe it?
- **Lies go far, and have a price**: a player can start a false rumour that NPCs hear, understand and use; NPCs lie too. When a lie is denied, trust falls along the chain, sometimes back to the liar.
- **A rumour can only say what a rule of the world can read** ("an army is coming": flee; "the ford is cursed": avoid it).
- Later, as decoration: rumours that warp from mouth to mouth, legends, nicknames, place names ("the Hanged Man's ford").

## 8. Groups

The fundamental building block of all society.

- A group has: **members, roles, powers per role, rules** (entry, exit, sanctions), **succession rule**.
- Groups can contain groups (empire > provinces) and have **relations** (alliance, war, vassalage, trade).
- **Public or secret**.
- Covers: tribes, families, guilds, brotherhoods of assassins, merchant congresses, religions, armies, empires, systems invented by players.
- **A role's powers are not magic locks**: they exist because members and NPCs recognize them. A general without the loyalty of their soldiers has nothing but a title.

## 9. Political power

- In the tribe: the chief is chosen by **strength and prestige**. Strength alone = quick but illegitimate and unstable power; prestige alone = legitimate but slow power; both = the most solid.
- Power is not tied to sex; players create their own norms.
- **No regime is coded** (12 November 2026). Monarchy, a council of players with NPCs, nomads, industrial towns, communities living with nature, herders, hunters, autarkies, an Alexander (player or NPC) who wants to colonise everything: all are born from the same pieces (groups, beliefs, choices, the land). Different lands make different societies: a closed valley stores grain and gets a king; herds make nomads; open forest keeps free bands.
- **NPCs equal to players**: a king has subjects even with few players; a council can have NPCs who vote according to their own minds.

## 10. Economy and logistics

- **Everything is physical**: transporting 500 loaves requires carts, horses, drivers, escorts.
- **Local markets.** No global auction house.
- **Spoilage is the fundamental engine** (confirmed 12 November 2026): food rots → it must be stored → one must discover how to keep it (smoking, drying, salting…) → stores become a stake: wars for food, famines, hunting, herding. The stored surplus is what gives birth to villages, chiefs and states.
- **The body that eats, drinks and carries** (13 November 2026; these rules hold for the game and are shown in the client, D34, D35): **thirst**, met at running water, and carrying water needs a container, so thirst sets where people camp; a **fat reserve**, so hunger is no longer a cliff: a body lives some days on its reserves, grows thin and weak, then dies; **cold** by season and hour, against clothes, shelter and fire. **Carrying without a container**: two objects in the hands, or one heavy object as the body's strength allows; a handful of loose things; beyond, a container; heavy loads tire over time. What a body carries is its own; the camp's store is the band's.
- Banditry, escorts, roads, inns: the economy creates trades.

## 11. Knowledge and technologies

- **Starting point**: advanced hunter-gatherers (fire, knapped stone, wood, ropes, spears, axes, hides, shelters).
- **No imposed tech tree.** Everything is possible if you gather the chain: knowledge, resources, labor, time.
- **Invention by assembly**: objects and buildings are made of parts; their qualities derive from the materials and the assembly.
- **Discovery** through experimentation and observation, emerging from the properties of the world (e.g. smoked meat keeps). Later, **intuitions** from skilled characters.
- **Knowledge as content** (12 November 2026, D15): concepts and processes with their prerequisites, at least two paths to each goal, the variants chosen by the maker.
- **Discovering**, NPCs and players alike: by a **surprise observed** (the meat kept longer than expected; bark soaked in water; children's play; seeing another's attempt succeed) or a **trial pushed by a need** (the cold, losses counted, the memory of last winter), and only **one step from what is already known**. Willpower is the **depth of the search**: how far back from the need a mind looks, among what the band has to hand. A trial costs time and materials taken from food.
- **Transmitting**: a skill is learnt by watching, working alongside, being taught; it degrades with each copy; it **dies with those who know it** if nobody learnt it.
- **Knowledge read on a wiki** lets a player try; the body decides whether it succeeds.
- Processes exist without being displayed: the space of possibilities is vast and hidden.
- In the long term, possibly AI to judge free-form inventions.
- **Knowing the land is knowledge too** (10 November 2026). The game shows **no map**: the world is huge, and players learn it by walking it and taking boats on its rivers (another arm, the far bank, the hills beyond). **The game organises nothing around it**: players land on the map and create their own needs, societies, food, the search for better places to live (an islet, water, shelter). Scouts, guides paid to lead others ("I know an iron mine, I'll take you there"), sellers of maps: these are roles players may invent, not systems of the game. Drawing maps that players can use is an idea for later. The movement serves this: a click takes the body by a way it can see, otherwise straight ahead until something stands in the way, and the player finds the rest (D31, D32).

## 12. World and end of the world

- World time: days of about 2 real hours, seasons of a few real days, childhood of a few weeks, a life of a few months (orders of magnitude to be adjusted).
- **The year** (13 November 2026, provisional): about 50 game days, four seasons, so a season lasts about a real day and a year about four; a life of sixty years about eight real months; two or three lives a real year, reborn as a child or in a body of 20, 30 or 50, often dying before old age. Coming back after a real month or two, one finds people 8 to 15 years older; after a real year, about ninety years have passed: the stone house, the stump, the name of the place remain. The value can still move and must be fixed before a world opens (`06`). Whatever is counted in days follows the day; whatever is counted in years follows the season (D09).
- **The world lives without players and is never frozen, but what nobody sees is not built** (12 November 2026): what nobody ever approached exists only as what is needed to make it (the world's seed, the land, the time elapsed, what happened around it). A huge continent, thousands of kilometres across; crossing it on foot takes real weeks.
- **Rock, caves and stone** (direction, 13 November 2026): rock shelters come before true caves; a cave will be a roofed area in the same map, like the inside of a house. A geology layer by region will drive caves (in limestone only), flint (chalk and limestone outcrops, river gravel), boulders and clay. It gets its own design page before it is built (`06`).
- **A world can die.** Nihilists could destroy humanity; it is extremely hard, and that is what gives meaning to the orders that fight them. Souls survive and migrate.
- **Core loop**: making the population prosper = more births = more bodies to be reborn into.
