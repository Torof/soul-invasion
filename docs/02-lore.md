# 02 — Lore

*Initial draft. The lore will be enriched as the project progresses.*

## An ordinary world

A world much like ours, with its own physical rules. **No magic.** The planet has its own rhythm: it spins quickly on its axis and around its sun, which gives shorter days and seasons than ours. Its inhabitants, **near-humans**, grow up faster and live shorter lives than we do. Nothing is "sped up": it is simply the nature of this world.

Each world (server) can have its own calendar and its own biology.

## The inhabitants: Willpower

The inhabitants **have no soul**. They are born, live and die; at death, they go out. What drives them is their **Willpower**, the will of the body.

- Willpower determines the **richness of their thought**: little Willpower, a simple, routine life with simple beliefs; much Willpower, ambition, plans, grudges, the capacity to rebel.
- Most inhabitants have modest Willpower; a few have exceptional Willpower.
- Willpower **can evolve**: an ordeal, an injustice, a responsibility or an education can make it grow.
- Low Willpower does not mean an empty shell: every inhabitant has a name, habits, moods, bonds.
- **Strong personalities** are few, fewer than one in fifty: the master craftsman who changes a workshop, the woman who holds a council, once or twice a generation someone who changes a kingdom. They do not make history out of nothing; they seize what the land and the times allow.
- **Willpower awakens.** Some are born with it, more often in hard times; most find it in the middle of their lives. A massacre, a famine, an injustice breaks most of those who live it up close, and forges a few: the peaceful peasant who comes out of the raid as the man the valley will follow. Nobody chooses them; the world only notices afterwards.

## The parasite souls

One day, **souls from elsewhere** appeared in this world. They are the players.

- They are not born: they **slip into a living body** whose Willpower leaves room for them.
- At death, they do not go out: they look for a new body.
- **Willpower resists the soul.** A child, a sick person, a broken slave yield easily; an ambitious chief or an educated noble resist. The stronger the Willpower, the more powerful a soul must be to seize it. This is the law that sets the cost of reincarnation.
- By taking a body, the soul inherits part of its **memories**: the names, the bonds, the places.
- **The origin of the souls is the world's great mystery.** Nobody knows where they come from or why.

## The Changed and suspicion

The inhabitants do not understand what is happening. They only notice that some people, after a fever or an accident, change: they speak differently, know impossible things, show a new ambition.

- **Understanding the Changed is a piece of knowledge the world discovers**, like a technology. In the first generations, people speak of sorcerers, of the possessed, of demons, without understanding the phenomenon. Only with an advanced civilization and many Changed will the fact be understood and named.
- **Suspicion** is born from what others notice. It is personal (everyone has their own doubts), and those close to you see the most.
- A Changed can learn **not to arouse suspicion**.

## Religion

Religion is born from the Changed, and becomes a major political, social and spiritual force.

- Some see the Changed as **envoys of the gods**, others as **demons** to be hunted.
- Depending on the region, being a Changed can be a blessing or a death sentence: inquisitions, hunts, spiritual wars.
- Religions bring legitimacy, laws, morality, and **rites tied to souls**: protection against possession, exorcisms, sanctuaries, consecrations of heirs.
- Players can **found their own religions**.

## The supernatural

The only supernatural thing in the world is **the souls**. Their influence remains rare and subtle: it acts at death, at rebirth, on the longevity of a body, on the aura of a sovereign. Nobody casts spells.

## The two summits

- **The Demon Lords** draw their power **from their soul**. A mystical path, often dark. Eight seats.
- **The World Emperor** draws their power **from others**. They remain mortal, but carry the **Aura of Destiny**, which grows with the number of those who recognize their authority. A single seat.
