# ground_water_03_summer

Sister of `ground_water_01`: the same calm cartoon water, a little paler and drawn in rounder cells with thin dark lines. Blended over 40 % of the river, turned a quarter turn on disk like every water image, its tone brought to the first water's by `sibling_tint` (6 November 2026).

- **File**: `ground_water_03_summer.png`, 1024 × 1024 (reduced from 2048), seamless; covers 4 × 4 m.
- **Generated** with Midjourney by the project lead, same references and prompt family as `ground_water_01`; the other tileable image of its batch of sixteen.
- **Measured**: mean RGB 90/162/158; edges continuous (2 × 2 tiling checked).
