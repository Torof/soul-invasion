# ground_water_01_summer

The river: hand-painted cartoon water in the tone of the style reference's river, deep teal with soft wavy lighter streaks of gentle current and a few small ripples, no foam, water from edge to edge. Third water of the day (6 November 2026): the first was a bright cyan with large cells, the second a flat dark teal that read as a painted ribbon.

What made it work: the **whole reference scene** as style reference (the `--sref` of the first grass), not the grass; a crop of the reference's river water as image reference; cartoon words, not technical ones; "current" avoided (it gives rapids), "gentle current" and "ripples" instead. Emerging stones are not in the image: they come as small separate images cut from the sister grid with stones (same water under them), placed here and there, never as a tile.

- **File**: `ground_water_01_summer.png`, 1024 × 1024 (reduced from 2048), seamless; covers 4 × 4 m.
- **Generated** with Midjourney by the project lead; chosen among eight (a sunken ruin, a drowned shape, parallel waves, and four with stones kept for cutting).
- **Prompt**: `seamless tileable top-down texture of river water in a cheerful hand-painted cartoon style, deep teal water with soft wavy lighter streaks of gentle current and a few small ripples painted in flat colours with thin outlines, water from edge to edge, no banks, no shore, no land, calm and even, flat lighting, game art texture --ar 1:1 --tile --sref https://cdn.midjourney.com/0fb23b62-fb63-43e8-8f0d-ccb4fdcf6751/0_3.png --sw 100 --iw 1.5 --s 50 --no rapids, whitewater, foam, waves, splashes, rocks, stones, banks, shore, sand, grass, trees, plants, fish, landscape, scenery, photo, realistic, shadows, vignette, text` (image reference: `reference-river-water.png`, a crop of the reference's river).
- **Measured**: mean RGB 44/137/126 (the reference's river: 59/122/104); edges continuous (2 × 2 tiling checked).
