# ground_water_01_summer

The river: hand-painted cartoon water in the tone of the style reference's river, mostly calm, large areas of flat teal with a few soft lighter streaks of gentle current, no foam, water from edge to edge. Fourth water of the day (6 November 2026): the first was a bright cyan with large cells, the second a flat dark teal that read as a painted ribbon, the third had streaks everywhere and too strong.

What made it work: the **whole reference scene** as style reference (the `--sref` of the first grass), not the grass; a crop of the reference's river water as image reference; cartoon words, not technical ones; "current" avoided (it gives rapids), "gentle current" and "ripples" instead. Emerging stones are not in the image: they come as small separate images cut from the sister grid with stones (same water under them), placed here and there, never as a tile.

- **File**: `ground_water_01_summer.png`, 1024 × 1024 (reduced from 2048), seamless; covers 4 × 4 m.
- **Generated** with Midjourney by the project lead; chosen among nine (foam and clouds, flagstones under water, a net of foam in cells; a swirling sister kept in reserve for places of strong current).
- **Prompt**: `seamless tileable top-down texture of mostly calm river water in a cheerful hand-painted cartoon style, deep teal, large calm areas of flat colour, with only a few soft lighter streaks of gentle current running from the top edge to the bottom edge of the frame, no diagonal, painted in flat colours with thin outlines, water from edge to edge, no banks, no shore, no land, even, flat lighting, game art texture --ar 1:1 --tile --sref https://cdn.midjourney.com/0fb23b62-fb63-43e8-8f0d-ccb4fdcf6751/0_3.png --sw 100 --iw 1.5 --s 50 --no rapids, whitewater, foam, waves, splashes, rocks, stones, banks, shore, sand, grass, trees, plants, fish, diagonal, landscape, scenery, photo, realistic, shadows, vignette, text` (image reference: `reference-river-water.png`, a crop of the reference's river). The streaks still came out diagonal.
- **Not edited**: an attempt to paint out the long bands by image processing read as blurred watercolour and was rejected; the image is used as generated. The long bands are to be removed in Midjourney's editor (erase the region, regenerate), not by hand.
- **Measured**: mean RGB 59/153/135 (the reference's river: 59/122/104), contrast 27; edges continuous (2 × 2 tiling checked).
