# ground_water_01_summer

The river: calm hand-painted cartoon water, flat teal plates in close tones, thin dark curved lines, small specks, a few short thin pale lines, no bands. Validated by the project lead on 6 November 2026 ("là on a vraiment ce qu'il faut"), the fifth water of the day.

What made it work, after four failures (bright cyan cells, flat dark ribbon, streaks everywhere, long white bands): a **screenshot of the good part of our own river** as both style and image reference, so the generator had nothing to copy but that water; and `--tile` typed in the prompt bar (it is dropped by Vary and the editor, which gave fourteen unusable images with a dark corner).

- **File**: `ground_water_01_summer.png`, 1024 × 1024 (reduced from 2048), seamless; covers 4 × 4 m.
- **Generated** with Midjourney by the project lead; chosen among sixteen as the calmest of the two tileable ones (7 % of bright pixels).
- **Prompt**: `seamless tileable top-down texture of calm river water exactly like the reference image, flat teal colour areas in two or three close tones, a few thin dark curved lines and small dark specks, no lighter streaks at all, no bands, even lighting over the whole image, no darker corner, hand-painted cartoon style with thin outlines, water from edge to edge, no banks, no shore, no objects, game art texture --ar 1:1 --tile --sw 100 --iw 2 --s 50 --no white streaks, pink highlights, peach, light bands, bright waves, foam, rapids, highlights, reflections, rocks, stones, pebbles, islands, banks, shore, sand, grass, plants, fish, landscape, scenery, photo, realistic, shadows, vignette, gradient, dark corner, text` (a screenshot of the river in the client as style and image reference).
- **Measured**: mean RGB 72/158/153, brought toward the reference's river by the tint in `ground.json`; edges continuous (2 × 2 tiling checked).
