# ground_water_02_summer

Calm water: deep dark teal, almost flat colour, no line. Too empty alone, it is the sister of `ground_water_01` (the cartoon water with streaks): blended over a little more than half of the river, it gives the calm stretches between the streaks (6 November 2026). The shader lifts its tone to the first water's (`sibling_tint`).

- **File**: `ground_water_02_summer.png`, 1024 × 1024 (reduced from 2048), seamless; covers 4 × 4 m.
- **Generated** with Midjourney by the project lead, with a crop of the reference's river water as image reference and `ground_grass_01` as style reference at half weight; the flattest of three.
- **Prompt**: `top-down seamless texture of deep calm river water filling the whole frame, dark teal blue-green exactly like the image reference, almost flat colour with a few soft lighter streaks painted in flat colour, no outlines, no banks, no shore, no edges, no objects, uniform and calm, seen exactly from above, flat even lighting, no highlights, very low contrast, flat cel-shaded game texture --ar 1:1 --tile --iw 2 --sw 50 --s 25 --no photo, realistic, bright, cyan, turquoise, ripples drawn as lines, waves, foam, highlights, sparkle, reflections, banks, shore, rocks, stones, pebbles, plants, fish, landscape, scenery, shadows, vignette, text`
- **Measured**: mean RGB 24/100/87, contrast 3; edges continuous.
