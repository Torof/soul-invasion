# ground_grass_06_summer

Sparse forest grass: thin blades and small low tufts standing upright, with bare earth showing between them. The floor of the woods (6 November 2026), with `ground_grass_05` as its sister and the worn grass in spots, all tinted to the shade by the shader.

- **File**: `ground_grass_06_summer.webp`, 1024 × 1024 (reduced from the 2048 the generator gave), seamless; covers 4 × 4 m. Imported with mipmaps since 10 November 2026 (D39), read a level sharper than the hardware would choose: as crisp up close, no shimmer when the camera moves.
- **Generated** with Midjourney by the project lead, with `ground_grass_01` attached as style reference; chosen among four (two had a visible seam, one had blades leaning as in wind).
- **Prompt**: `top-down seamless ground texture, sparse short forest grass, thin scattered blades and small low tufts, bare dark earth showing between them over about half of the surface, the grass a little duller green than a meadow, uniform and calm, seen exactly from above, no perspective, flat even lighting with no vignette and no shadows, very low contrast, thin subtle outlines only, drawn in the same flat cel-shaded style as the reference, game texture --ar 1:1 --tile --sw 100 --s 50 --no photo, realistic, dense grass, bushes, shrubs, moss, leaves, mushrooms, branches, sticks, rocks, pebbles, flowers, plants, ferns, trees, animals, objects, autumn, orange, red, shadows, vignette, text`
- **Measured**: mean RGB 93/134/61 against 90/133/61 for the first grass; edges continuous (checked on a 2 × 2 tiling).
