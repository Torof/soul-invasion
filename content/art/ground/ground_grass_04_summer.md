# ground_grass_04_summer

A second sister of `ground_grass_01`, more open: the same meadow grass with a few patches of shorter, flatter grass between the tufts. Blended in soft patches over the first grass to vary the texture of the meadow without changing its tone (6 November 2026).

- **File**: `ground_grass_04_summer.webp`, 1024 × 1024, seamless; covers 4 × 4 m. Imported with mipmaps since 10 November 2026 (D39), read a level sharper than the hardware would choose: as crisp up close, no shimmer when the camera moves.
- **Generated** with Midjourney by the project lead on 6 November 2026, with `ground_grass_01` attached as style reference, from the prompt of `ground_grass_01`; chosen among nine sisters for its open patches.
- **Measured**: mean RGB 88/127/61 against 90/133/61 for the first grass; flat lighting; edges continuous (checked on a 2 × 2 tiling).
