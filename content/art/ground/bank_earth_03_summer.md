# bank_earth_03_summer

Damp packed brown earth of a river bank, seen from above, seamless; the bank's ordinary matter, laid by the ground shader between the water and the grass (ShoreField).

- **File**: `bank_earth_03_summer.webp`, 1024 × 1024, seamless both ways; 4 m a texture, like the ground. Imported with mipmaps since 10 November 2026 (D39), read a level sharper than the hardware would choose: as crisp up close, no shimmer when the camera moves.
