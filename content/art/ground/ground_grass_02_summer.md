# ground_grass_02_summer

Meadow grass worn by footsteps: the second validated ground (6 November 2026), mixed with `ground_grass_01` block by block to break the four-metre repetition.

- **File**: `ground_grass_02_summer.webp`, 1024 × 1024, seamless; covers 4 × 4 m. Imported with mipmaps since 10 November 2026 (D39), read a level sharper than the hardware would choose: as crisp up close, no shimmer when the camera moves.
- **Generated** with Midjourney by the project lead, with `ground_grass_01` attached as the first style reference and the scene reference second; chosen among four (two had shrubs or a vignette, one was too bright).
- **Prompt**: `top-down seamless ground texture, short even meadow grass, worn by footsteps in places with a few small patches of bare dirt, drawn in flat cel shading with thin subtle outlines, uniform and calm, seen exactly from above, flat even lighting, no vignette, no shadows, game texture --ar 1:1 --tile --sw 100 --no photo, realistic, bushes, shrubs, trees, flowers, rocks, objects, shadows, vignette, text` (style references attached in the Midjourney app).
- **Measured**: centre 102, corners 98–114; edges continuous.
