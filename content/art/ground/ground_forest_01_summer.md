# ground_forest_01_summer

The floor of the groves: dark brown soil with small plaques of moss, the first forest ground (6 November 2026).

- **File**: `ground_forest_01_summer.png`, 1024 × 1024, seamless; covers 4 × 4 m.
- **Generated** with Midjourney by the project lead, with `ground_grass_01` attached as style reference; chosen among eight from two grids. The first grid (moss as cushions reading as shrubs, large orange leaves) was rejected whole: when an element is named, the prompt must also say its dose.
- **Prompt**: `top-down seamless ground texture, mostly plain dark brown forest soil, almost bare, with only a few small scattered dead leaves covering about a tenth of the surface, and two or three small faint patches of green moss, everything subtle and understated, uniform and calm, seen exactly from above, no perspective, flat even lighting with no vignette and no shadows, very low contrast, thin subtle outlines only, drawn in the same flat cel-shaded style as the reference, game texture --ar 1:1 --tile --sw 100 --s 50 --no photo, realistic, mushrooms, branches, sticks, logs, roots, rocks, pebbles, pinecones, acorns, flowers, plants, ferns, grass, trees, animals, objects, autumn, orange, red, shadows, vignette, text`
- **Measured**: mean RGB 112/100/59, the lowest contrast of its grid; edges continuous (checked on a 2 × 2 tiling). The moss plaques are spread evenly, so a sister without moss is wanted to break their rhythm.
