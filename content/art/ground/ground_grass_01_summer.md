# ground_grass_01_summer

The meadow grass of the valley: the first validated ground (6 November 2026).

- **File**: `ground_grass_01_summer.webp`, 1024 × 1024, seamless; covers 4 × 4 m (256 px per metre, enough for zoom ×4). Imported with mipmaps since 10 November 2026 (D39), read a level sharper than the hardware would choose: as crisp up close, no shimmer when the camera moves.
- **Generated** with Midjourney by the project lead, chosen among four variations (the dullest, as the rule says).
