# ground_water_04_summer

Swirling sister of `ground_water_01`: the same cartoon water with concentric swirls. Not calm, but compatible (tone within six points, seamless): the river's accent, blended over a quarter of it with a medium fade (6 November 2026). Turned a quarter turn on disk, like every water image, so its lines follow the river.

- **File**: `ground_water_04_summer.png`, 1024 × 1024 (reduced from 2048), seamless; covers 4 × 4 m.
- **Generated** with Midjourney by the project lead, same references as `ground_water_01`; one of two tileable images in a batch of seven.
- **Measured**: mean RGB 61/157/147; edges continuous (2 × 2 tiling checked).
