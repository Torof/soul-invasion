# ground_water_05_summer

The calm water: the same cartoon water nearly flat, fine ripples along the current, a quarter of the first water's detail, same tone within three points. The river's calm stretches, blended over about a fifth of it with a wide fade (`worn_softness` 0.35), which is what makes it melt into the lively water (6 November 2026). Turned a quarter turn on disk so its ripples follow the river.

Tried as the river's base with the lively water in zones: too calm, and the calm read as another pattern. As a few stretches with a wide fade it is right.

- **File**: `ground_water_05_summer.png`, 1024 × 1024 (reduced from 2048), seamless; covers 4 × 4 m.
- **Generated** with Midjourney by the project lead, with a crop of `ground_water_01` as style and image reference and `--s 25`; the calmest tileable image of its batch.
- **Measured**: mean RGB 71/157/161; edges continuous (2 × 2 tiling checked).
