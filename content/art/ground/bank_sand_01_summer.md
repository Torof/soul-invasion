# bank_sand_01_summer

The river bank: a strip of pale painted sand with its wet darker edge and a hint of grass, cut from a tileable Midjourney image of a river between two banks (6 November 2026). The shader lays it in the water's one-tile spill over the land: the wet edge against the water, the grass side fading into our grass. Repeats along the river (the strip is a vertical cut of a `--tile` image, so it tiles along its height).

Why a cut: with `--tile` Midjourney cannot put a bank on one side and water on the other (the edges must meet), so it draws water on both sides with the land in the middle; a vertical strip of that is a bank.

- **File**: `bank_sand_01_summer.png`, 310 × 1024 (cut 620 × 2048 from columns 1180–1800 of the source, mirrored so x = 0 is the water side), repeats along y; 4 m along the river, about 1.2 m across.
- **Generated** with Midjourney by the project lead (`cartoon river bank on the left side taking one third…`, with `--tile`); chosen among four as the cleanest sand (no rocks, no bushes).
- **Source**: kept in `content/ai-reserve/reserve/` (`6bb0…_3`).
